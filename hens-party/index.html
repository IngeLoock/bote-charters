Hens Party on the Water

Give the bride-to-be a day she will never forget. A hens party aboard Bote is champagne on the bow, cocktails at the bar, a swim stop in the bay and a dance floor as the sun goes down. The whole yacht is yours, so bring the decorations, the playlist and your favourite people.

What's Included

Her day, your way

Tell us the vibe, from a relaxed long lunch to full party mode, and we will shape the route, the music and the menu around it. Planning the bucks too? See our hens and bucks page.

  • Exclusive use of the whole 80ft yacht
  • Up to 60 guests across 3 areas
  • Cocktail and champagne bar service
  • Swim stops at Wavebreak Island or Moreton Bay
  • Decorations, sashes and DJ bookings welcome
  • Grazing platters and catering packages
