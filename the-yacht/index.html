Practical

Good to know.

Still have a question? Call 0413 404 518 or send us a message. We reply within 24 hours.

Is there shade and air conditioning?
The aft deck is covered by a bimini and the saloon and cabins below are air conditioned. The bow is open sun.
Can we swim?
Yes, at anchor in Moreton Bay and the Broadwater, at the skipper’s call. Lifejackets are aboard. Bring towels.
Is there a bathroom?
Yes, below deck, with a shower.
Music?
Bluetooth sound system through the deck. Bring the playlist; a DJ can be arranged for larger events.
Decorations?
Balloons, florals and signage are welcome. No confetti or glitter. Set up is in the fifteen minutes before departure.
Shoes?
Shoes off on board, to protect the teak.
