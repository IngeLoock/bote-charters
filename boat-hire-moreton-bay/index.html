Moreton Bay charters

Good to know.

Still have a question? Call 0413 404 518 or send us a message. We reply within 24 hours.

How long does it take to get out there?
Allow a longer charter for a bay day. Six hours gives you a proper stretch at anchor; the skipper plans the timing with you.
Can we swim?
Yes, at anchor, at the skipper’s call. The swim platform, inflatables and lifejackets are all on board. Bring towels and sunscreen.
Where do we board?
In Brisbane, at New Farm Park or the City Botanic Gardens River Hub. The cruise down the river is part of the day.
What time can we start?
Choose from three charter times: 10am to 2pm, 2:30 to 6:30pm or 7 to 11pm. For a bay day, the morning or afternoon charter with extra hours gives you the longest stretch at anchor.
