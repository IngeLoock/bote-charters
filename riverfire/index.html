Questions

Before you book.

Still have a question? Call 0413 404 518 or send us a message. We reply within 24 hours.

When is Riverfire?
Riverfire closes Brisbane Festival each year, usually in late September. The yacht takes one group, and it books out months ahead.
How many can we bring?
Up to 60. Above 40 an additional crew member joins at $75 per hour, and above 50 at $150 per hour.
Can we bring our own drinks?
Bote is a licensed vessel, so all drinks come from our bar: the drinks package, a bar tab settled on board, or pay as you go. Tell us your favourites and we’ll stock them.
What time do we board?
Early. The river fills with boats from mid-afternoon, so we depart ahead of the crowd and anchor with the bridge in view.
