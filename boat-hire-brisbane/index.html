The River City from the Water

Boat Hire Brisbane

See Brisbane from its most scenic perspective. Board at New Farm Park River Hub or the City Botanic Gardens River Hub and glide under the Story Bridge, past the Kangaroo Point cliffs, South Bank Parklands and the sparkling skyline, all from the deck of your own private 80ft yacht.

On the Route

The Brisbane River cruise

Easy pick-up and drop-off from New Farm Park River Hub or the City Botanic Gardens River Hub. Take in the striking contrast between the vibrant city skyline and the charm of the riverside suburbs. By evening, the shimmering city lights dance on the water, creating a magical view of Brisbane.

Further Afield

Out to Moreton Bay

Just 40 kilometres off the coast, Moreton Island is a paradise of crystal-clear waters, stunning beaches and the iconic Tangalooma Wrecks. Add Peel Island, Cowan Cowan or Coochiemudlo to the route for swim stops, snorkelling and beachside relaxation.
